## Source checked

The official Khronos glTF Sample Assets repository publishes **Materials Variants Shoe**, copyright Shopify (2021), under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**, with a downloadable GLB and three material variants. Its source README was retrieved and checked during this continuation:

https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/MaterialsVariantsShoe

This is a generic shoe sample, not a verified match for the seven named products or their colourways. It is not adopted as their product asset. An available commercial-use sample does not fulfil the product-specific requirement.

The Sketchfab asset-search API was also attempted, but this executor's outbound proxy rejects `api.sketchfab.com`. No assertion is made that the seven exact models are available for free or that a marketplace result has been licence-verified. Marketplace browsing and source verification require destination access; existing merchant-owned or supplier-authorised assets can also be evaluated.

## Acceptance record for each adopted asset

Record the original asset URL, creator, exact licence/version, required attribution, download date and file checksum. Verify the actual sneaker silhouette, construction and colourway against approved product photography. Keep any licence notices with the files, test texture/geometry cost on mobile, and upload accepted assets as Shopify product media. Reject missing licences, non-commercial licences and product mismatches.

Existing `public/reference` imagery remains explicitly illustrative and limited to the design-review route. No new product asset has been downloaded or relabelled in this sourcing pass.
